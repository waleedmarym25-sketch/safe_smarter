# SAFE - Period Tracker & Female Care Intelligence Platform 🌸👑

A female health platform and menstrual cycle tracker featuring:
- 🌸 **Smart Period & Bleeding Flow Tracking:** Record dates, cycle length, period duration, pain intensity (1-10), pain locations, and bleeding volume (spotting, light, medium, heavy, clots).
- ⭐ **Ovulation & Fertility Intelligence:** Automatically predicts peak ovulation windows and tracks fertile symptoms.
- 🔔 **5-Day Advance Alerts:** Pre-period advance warnings with self-care checklists and audio chimes.
- 💬 **Empathetic AI Health Copilot:** Multilingual AI assistant providing kind, compassionate medical advice on safe painkillers and natural comfort remedies.
- 🌐 **5 Complete Languages:** Full instantaneous switching for Arabic (🇸🇦), English (🇬🇧), French (🇫🇷), German (🇩🇪), and Swahili (🇹🇿).
- 🎨 **5 Luxury Themes:** Executive Gold, Rose Pink, Royal Purple, Ocean Teal, and Emerald Green with Dark Mode.
- 📲 **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Installable directly on iPhone, Android, and Desktop.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5 / CSS3 / ES Modules (Vite & PWA)
- **Backend:** Node.js & Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (`SAFE`)
- **Security:** JWT Authentication & Bcrypt Password Hashing
